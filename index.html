The franchise operations partner

Built for the reality of running a gym franchise

Managing people in a fitness franchise is genuinely complex. Casual workforces, high turnover, shift-based rosters, Award compliance, and the pressure of running a business where your staff are your product — it demands more than generic HR software.

World Gym Australia selected Employment Hero as its franchise operations partner on the software front because it covers every stage of the employment lifecycle in one system — with Australian compliance infrastructure built in by default.
